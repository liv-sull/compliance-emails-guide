One-off change: send from the Learning mailbox

Do this once in your flow. After that, every draft comes from Learning.

  1. Go to make.powerautomate.com and click My flows.
  2. Click Compliance Manager Emails, then Edit at the top.
  3. Click the box called Draft an email message.
  4. Click Show all (it may say Advanced parameters) and choose From (Send as).
  5. Type the Learning mailbox address in the From (Send as) field.
  6. Leave the To field alone. It must still show ManagerEmail.
  7. Click Save.
